# Compatibility

- **Harmony** required.
- **[FSF] Complex Jobs:** supported. Search and headers use `labelShort` (Nurse, Surgeon). Load Complex Jobs first.
- **Automated Work Assignment:** incompatible (same Work grid).
- Other Work-tab replacements (Fluffy Work Tab, etc.): likely UI conflict.
- Biotech optional: gene conditions and Childcare no-op without the DLC.
